How to film your swing⌄
  • Face-on: camera square to your chest, in line with the ball.
  • Hip height, 3–4 m away, phone upright (portrait). Tripod or lean it on your bag.
  • Whole body, the ball and a bit of space above the club at the top in frame.
  • Use slow-mo or 60 fps if you can. Start filming 2 s before you set up.
  • One swing per clip. Good light, no one walking behind you.
